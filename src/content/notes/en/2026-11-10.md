## New

### Dig up a bush
- The window of a berry bush, full or already picked, has a **"Dig up the stump"** button.
- A small shovel spins above the marked bush. A gatherer comes and digs it out.
- They bring back a **stump**, a new resource stored at the **berry hut** (up to 20, apart from the 60 berries).

### Plant bushes
- The berry hut's card has a **"Plant a bush"** button.
- Place bushes like a building, inside the hut's circle: the tile turns green when it is free.
- A gatherer takes a stump from the hut and plants it. The new bush fills with berries at the start of summer.

### Time panel
- To the right of the season strip: **play, pause, slow down, speed up**.
- Six speeds: ×0.5, ×1, ×2, ×5, ×10 and ×20.

## Improvements
- **Right-clicking any bush**, full or picked, makes the selected villagers gatherers, even without a berry hut. They dig up the stump if it is marked, pick berries if any are left, or else look for work nearby.
