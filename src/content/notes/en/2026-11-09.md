## New

### Health points
- Each villager has **100 health points**, shown on their card with a heart.
- Hunger, cold and age no longer kill: only health points matter.

### Woodcutting workshop
- A villager can be **assigned to the workshop**: the workstation is reserved for them.
- Two separate stocks: **20 logs** and **40 firewood**. Each log gives 2 firewood.

## Improvements
- **Foresters first pick up the logs on the ground** before chopping or planting. No more chopping while no storage has room.
- A forester assigned to a Ranger's Cabin with nothing else to do **plants right away**, without waiting for the cabin.
- The **Ranger's Cabin** is smaller (4 × 4 tiles) and its tower reaches the treetops.
- The **views menu** opens with the eye button, top right. Several views can be on at once: in colour when on, black and white otherwise.
- New **parchment background** for buttons throughout the game.
