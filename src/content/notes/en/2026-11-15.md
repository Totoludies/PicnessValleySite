## New

### Villagers who all look different
- Four outfits (blue, yellow, red, green), each in a **male and female** version.
- Every villager rolls a **hairstyle** (four per sex), a **hair colour**, an **eye colour**, and a first name matching their sex.
- The blue villager wears a floppy hat that sways as they move.
- Four counters under the season strip count the villagers of each colour.

### Children
- A villager born in a house starts out tiny and **grows up until age 4**. They take small steps, a little faster to keep up with the grown-ups.
- Children don't work: no job and no task before age 4.
- Age is counted in season years and shown on the villager's card.

### Health
- **Hunger** and **cold** drain health, three times faster when both add up. Villagers heal once they are fed and warm again.
- A villager who dies lies on the ground for a while, then their body slowly fades away.
- A **heart button** in the views menu shows health bars above villagers.

### The dragon
- A **dragon** lives on the map. It waits, sleeps, takes off and flies a **figure eight** over the start flag before settling down again.
- It can land on top of plateaus.

### Villager card
- A **live portrait** shows the villager's face, with their activity next to it and their job and house below.

### Map editor
- New look in the game's style: picture buttons for the tools, a **Brush / Eraser** panel with size and density, and a **Settings** wheel (load, save, save as) that Esc also opens.
- **Cursor** tool to pick trees, bushes, rocks, the dragon and the start with a click or a box, then rotate or delete them (Del).
- New scenery: snowy **mountains**, cliff-edged **plateaus**, and **tall grass** that turns yellow in autumn and bends in the wind.
- Maps come in **several sizes**, up to five times the area, all playable.
- A map needs a start and a dragon before it can be saved.

### New default map
- The **"Classic"** map, three times larger than the old one, replaces "Picness Valley".

## Improvements

### Building
- The chosen building arrives **with its door facing the camera**; a reminder of the **R** key to rotate it shows next to the ghost.
- **Green arrows** on the ground show building entrances while placing them and when their card is open.
- The tool puts itself away after placing; hold **Shift** to place several.
- An ordered building stays a ghost until construction starts, and ordered roads are drawn as ghosts.
- You can't build on an ordered road that hasn't been laid yet.

### The valley
- A felled tree leaves **five logs** lined up along its fall.
- **Spring flowers** in white, yellow and pink, which wilt at the end of summer ("Flowers" slider in the graphics settings).
- Footprints in the snow.
- A new paved **Logistics Center**, and redrawn paved roads.

### Comfort
- **Wooden cursors**: an arrow, and a hammer while building.
- The camera **scrolls when the mouse touches the edge** of the screen.

### How villagers get around
- Villagers **follow the roads**, even if it means a small detour, and walk down the middle.
- They **walk around buildings** and storage areas, and **no longer cut across fields**: only farmers step in to work.
- They go in and out of buildings **through the door**: houses, huts, barn, market, small storage area.
- When strolling, they no longer wander into the middle of a field or a stockpile.
- They tire **half as fast** while working.
- No more spinning shovel above construction sites and roads to be built.

### Browser version
- A **Totoludies** loading screen with its progress bar, instead of a black screen.
- The game uses **WebGPU** when the browser supports it, with about 20% less work per frame. Other browsers play in WebGL 2 as before.
