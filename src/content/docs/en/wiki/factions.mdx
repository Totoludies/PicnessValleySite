As your village grows, you must make sure that enough of your villagers are ready to sacrifice themselves to appease the dragon [Zebor](/en/wiki/dragon/). But everyone sees the world their own way: your villagers belong to **four schools of thought**.

Each school has its **colour**, its **emblem** and its **outfit**. A villager is born into one of the four, at random. In the game, four counters under the season strip show how many living villagers belong to each school, in the order blue triangle, yellow flame, green circle, red square.

## Outfits

Each school has its own outfit, easy to spot from afar in the village. The animations are the game's own: pick one under the model.

| School | Outfit |
|---|---|
| Deists (blue) | Tall pointed hat tilted back, lowered hood, cape and pleated belted robe. The hat is soft and follows every move. |
| Liberals (yellow) | Two-horned crescent hat on a skullcap, high collar, pointed cape at the back and turned-up sleeves. |
| Bayesians (green) | Bowler hat with a rolled brim, wide open collar, loose robe and very large turned-up sleeves. |
| Visionaries (red) | Tousled hair, wide headband tied at the back, shirt with a pointed collar and belted trousers. |

## Strengths and strategies
Each community has its strengths and weaknesses. Buildings have synergies with each other, and several strategies are possible: specialise in one faction or combine communities.

:::danger[Beware]
Too many Visionaries will doom you for sure.
:::

:::note[In development]
For now, a villager's school changes their outfit and the counters, but not yet their behaviour. Each school's strengths are coming in a future version; this wiki will be completed then.
:::
