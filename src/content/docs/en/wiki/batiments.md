Buildings are placed from the **build bar** on the left of the screen: hover a category to see its buildings, with their cost and build time.

A placed building becomes a **building site**: villagers clear the ground, bring the materials, then build. Every building can be paused or demolished from its card (nothing is refunded when demolishing).

:::note[Costs]
The exact cost of each building will soon be taken straight from the game. Meanwhile, they are shown in the build bar.
:::
