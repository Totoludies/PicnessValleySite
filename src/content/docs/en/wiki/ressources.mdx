:::

## On the map
### Trees
<Model3D
	title="Tree"
	variants={[
		{ src: 'tree', label: 'Grown tree' },
		{ src: 'young-tree', label: 'Young tree' },
	]}
	caption="Through the seasons, trees blossom, turn yellow or red, shed their leaves and get covered in snow."
/>

A felled grown tree gives **5 logs**, left on the ground in the direction of its fall and then stored. Young trees grow for **one year** before they can be cut. A new young tree sprouts at the start of every summer.

### Rocks
<Model3D title="Rock" src="rock" caption="In winter, the stone turns white and snow caps grow on top." />

| Rock | Stones |
|---|---|
| Large | 10 |
| Medium | 5 |
| Small | 1 |

Each harvest gives 1 stone, and the rock **shrinks** as it is broken. It disappears once empty.

### Berry bushes
<Model3D
	title="Berry bush and stump"
	variants={[
		{ src: 'berry-bush', label: 'Bush' },
		{ src: 'stump', label: 'Stump' },
	]}
/>

- A full bush holds **5 berries**; each picking takes 1, and you can see the berries disappear one by one.
- An empty bush fills up again at the **start of summer**. A new bush grows about every other summer.
- From its window, a bush can be **dug up** ("Dig up the stump" button): a gatherer brings a stump back to the Berry Hut.
- From a Berry Hut's card, **"Plant a bush"** places new bushes inside its circle. They start without berries.

### Tall grass
<Model3D title="Tall grass" src="tall-grass" caption="Green in spring and summer, yellow in autumn, flattened under the snow in winter." />

Tufts of tall grass decorate the map. They sway in the wind and follow the seasons, but cannot be harvested.

Clicking a tree, a rock or a bush opens its window, showing what it has left.
