A villager with no job only does everyone's tasks: picking up, carrying, building. To give them a job, **right-click** what they should do: a tree makes them a forester, a rock a miner, a building site a carpenter. They then keep that job and work on their own.

| | Job | What they do |
|---|---|---|
| <img class="pv-icon" src="/icons/jobs/forester.webp" alt="" /> | **Forester** | First picks up logs on the ground, then cuts trees. Assigned to a Ranger's Cabin, they cut and replant inside its circle. |
| <img class="pv-icon" src="/icons/jobs/gatherer.webp" alt="" /> | **Gatherer** | Picks berries. Also digs up the stumps of marked bushes and plants the bushes placed around a Berry Hut. |
| <img class="pv-icon" src="/icons/jobs/miner.webp" alt="" /> | **Miner** | Breaks rocks into stone. |
| <img class="pv-icon" src="/icons/jobs/builder.webp" alt="" /> | **Carpenter** | Builds buildings, from clearing to the last plank. |
| <img class="pv-icon" src="/icons/jobs/woodcutter.webp" alt="" /> | **Woodcutter** | Splits logs into firewood in the Woodcutting Workshop. Does not cut trees. |
| <img class="pv-icon" src="/icons/jobs/farmer.webp" alt="" /> | **Farmer** | Works the potato fields. |
| <img class="pv-icon" src="/icons/jobs/peddler.webp" alt="" /> | **Logistician** | Brings food and firewood to houses connected to the Logistics Center by road. |

## Assigning villagers to a building
Right-clicking some buildings **assigns** the selected villagers to them:

| Building | Places | Job given |
|---|---|---|
| Ranger's Cabin | 3 | Forester |
| Berry Hut | 2 | Gatherer |
| Woodcutting Workshop | 1 | Woodcutter |

Assigned foresters only work inside their cabin's circle. They replant depending on how many trees the circle holds: 2 plantings after each cut below 80 trees, 1 from 80 to 99, none from 100 on.

The **Jobs** menu, top right, counts the villagers in each job.
