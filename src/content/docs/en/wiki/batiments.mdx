## From building site to building
A placed building becomes a **building site**, which goes through three steps. Its card shows the progress ("Construction: N %") and one gauge per material to bring.

| Step | What happens | Share of progress |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Clearing | Trees, rocks and bushes on the footprint are removed, then a shovel clears each tile. | 20 % |
| 2. Delivery | Villagers bring the materials (a wheelbarrow spins above the site). | 30 % |
| 3. Construction | Carpenters put up the building, which rises from the ground. | 50 % |

- While placing, the **R key** rotates the building; it starts with its door facing the camera. Keep **Shift** held when releasing the click to place several in a row.
- A **green arrow** on the ground shows each entrance while placing and while the card is open.
- Nothing can be placed on an ordered road that has not been laid yet.
- Every building can be **paused** (its tasks stop, its villagers lose their home) or **demolished** from its card. **Nothing is refunded** when demolishing: neither materials nor contents.

## Roads
| | Road | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| <img class="pv-icon" src="/icons/buildings/DirtRoad.webp" alt="" /> | **Dirt Road** | Free | Villagers walk **10 % faster** on it. |
| <img class="pv-icon" src="/icons/buildings/PavedRoad.webp" alt="" /> | **Paved Road** | 1 stone per tile | Villagers walk **20 % faster** on it. |

Villagers take a detour along roads when it saves time. A road ending in front of a door extends up to it. Each road tile is demolished from its own card.

**Simple House** · <span class="pv-cost">10 <img class="pv-res" src="/icons/items/log.webp" alt="logs" /></span> · 5 × 5 tiles

- Houses villagers and brings **new ones** into the world.
- Keeps **5 firewood and 5 food** in total. Villagers eat and warm up there.
- The fire burns while there is wood and the season is cold: the chimney smokes, and wood burns faster the colder the season.
- In winter, snow covers the thatch in patches, with icicles along the roof's edge.

## Storage

<Model3D title="Small Storage Area" src="small-storage" />

**Small Storage Area** · free · 3 × 3 tiles

- Stores **stone, logs and firewood**: one pile of 4 items per tile, 9 piles in all, visible on their tile.
- **No materials** to bring: clearing only. Villagers can walk through it.
- An opening in the middle of the front side; a road ending in front of it extends up to it.

<Model3D title="Small Cellar" src="small-cellar" />

**Small Cellar** · <span class="pv-cost">5 <img class="pv-res" src="/icons/items/log.webp" alt="logs" /></span> <span class="pv-cost">10 <img class="pv-res" src="/icons/items/stone.webp" alt="stones" /></span> · 3 × 6 tiles

- Keeps **100 food** in total, berries and potatoes alike.
- A stone cellar under a vault of planks, with its door on a short side.

## Food

**Potato Field** · free · 2 × 2 to 10 × 10 tiles

- Drawn as a rectangle, at the size you want.
- **Farmers** plough it in spring, it grows in summer, they harvest it in autumn; it is reset in winter.

<Model3D title="Berry Hut" src="berry-hut" />

**Berry Hut** · <span class="pv-cost">10 <img class="pv-res" src="/icons/items/log.webp" alt="logs" /></span> <span class="pv-cost">5 <img class="pv-res" src="/icons/items/stone.webp" alt="stones" /></span> · 4 × 4 tiles

- Up to **2 gatherers** attached; its work circle has a **25-tile** radius.
- Keeps **60 berries**, visible in twelve baskets on its deck, and separately **20 stumps**. Villagers come here to eat.
- Its **"Plant a bush"** button places new bushes inside its circle: a gatherer plants a stump there, which becomes a bush without berries.

## Resources

<Model3D title="Ranger's Cabin" src="ranger-cabin" />

**Ranger's Cabin** · <span class="pv-cost">20 <img class="pv-res" src="/icons/items/log.webp" alt="logs" /></span> · 4 × 4 tiles

- Up to **3 foresters** attached; its work circle has a **30-tile** radius. Its card counts the trees in the circle.
- Attached foresters pick up, cut and **replant** only inside the circle, at a pace that depends on the number of trees:

| Trees in the circle | Plantings before the next cut |
|---|---|
| Fewer than 80 | 2 |
| 80 to 99 | 1 |
| 100 or more | none: they cut freely |

## Crafting

<Model3D title="Woodcutting Workshop" src="woodcutting-workshop" />

**Woodcutting Workshop** · <span class="pv-cost">10 <img class="pv-res" src="/icons/items/log.webp" alt="logs" /></span> · 4 × 4 tiles

- A **woodcutter** at the chopping block turns **1 log into 2 firewood**.
- Two separate stocks: **20 logs** and **40 firewood**, visible under the lean-to.
- Its card sets the village's **global firewood limit** (40 by default, no maximum). It also counts the wood in houses and other storage, and stops the splitting once reached.
- The axe stuck in the block disappears while the woodcutter works there.
