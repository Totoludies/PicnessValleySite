A **year lasts 4 minutes** at normal speed: four one-minute seasons, each in three parts (early, mid, late). The strip at the top of the screen scrolls through the year, and a notification announces each new season.

## Cold
The colder the season, the faster villagers lose warmth and the more firewood houses burn.

| Season | Cold |
|---|---|
| Spring | 1 |
| Summer | 0 |
| Autumn | 1 |
| Winter | 2 |

In summer, no fire burns: chimneys do not smoke.

## What changes through the year
### Spring
Snow melts in patches, trees grow their leaves back then blossom, and petals fall at the end of the season. Fields are ploughed.

### Summer
Empty bushes fill with berries, a young tree sprouts, and sometimes a new bush. Potato fields grow.

### Autumn
Trees turn yellow or red, each in its own shade, and lose their leaves. The wind picks up. Potatoes are harvested.

### Winter
Snow covers the ground, roofs, rocks and roads. Villagers leave footprints in the snow on the roads. Fields are reset. This is the season when firewood matters most.

:::tip
Change the game speed from the time panel, right of the season strip: ×0.5, ×1, ×2, ×5, ×10 or ×20.
:::
