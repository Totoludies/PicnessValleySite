Each villager has their own name, height and character. They live their life on their own: you give them a job or an order, they take care of the rest. Their outfit depends on their [school of thought](/en/wiki/factions/), picked at random at birth.

One villager in two is a woman: she wears the same outfit, with a bust and long hair. Pick the school and the sex in the viewer.

The starting villagers are 18 to 30 years old. A villager born in a house starts at 0, a quarter of an adult's height, and grows until 4, trotting along with quick little steps. A child does not work: their card shows "Child" instead of a job. The viewer's age slider shows them at any age.

## Gauges
| | Gauge | What to know |
|---|---|---|
| <img class="pv-icon" src="/icons/needs/health.webp" alt="" /> | **Health** | 100 per villager. Reaching 0 is the only cause of death. |
| <img class="pv-icon" src="/icons/needs/food.webp" alt="" /> | **Food** | Goes down all the time. The villager eats 1 food when coming home. Below 30 %, they lose health. |
| <img class="pv-icon" src="/icons/needs/cold.webp" alt="" /> | **Warmth** | Goes down faster as the season gets colder. They warm up at home if there is firewood. Below 30 %, they lose health. |
| <img class="pv-icon" src="/icons/needs/energy.webp" alt="" /> | **Energy** | Work is tiring. They rest to get it back. |

:::caution[Villagers can die]
Starving **or** freezing (gauge below 30 %), a villager loses 0.5 health per second; starving **and** freezing, three times faster. They get it back when food and warmth are both at 50 % or more. Details are on the [Game mechanics](/en/wiki/mecaniques/) page.
:::

## How a villager decides what to do
In this order:
1. **Your order**, unless they are very hungry, very cold or out of energy.
2. If they carry items, they **drop them off** in a storage.
3. Very hungry (below 40 %): they **go home to eat**.
4. Very cold (below 40 %): they **go home to warm up**, and fetch wood if the house has none left.
5. Out of energy: they **rest**.
6. Fewer than 5 firewood at home: they **go and fetch some**.
7. They **work**: the closest task for their job, otherwise a task with no job (picking up, building).
8. A little hungry or cold (below 60 %): they go home.
9. Energy not full: they rest.
10. Nothing to do: they stroll near their home.

## A villager's card
Clicking a villager opens their card: name, age, job, current activity, their four gauges (health, energy, food, warmth), the item they carry and a button to their house. The card closes if they die.
