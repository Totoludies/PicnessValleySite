The valley of Picness is guarded by **Zebor, the invincible dragon**. In exchange for the right to settle here, he demands to be fed human lives. If he is not satisfied, he will raze your village. It is up to you to organise your society so that your villagers agree to the ultimate sacrifice: that is what the [schools of thought](/en/wiki/factions/) are all about.

## His life in the valley
Zebor has his own spot on the map, sometimes on top of a plateau. There he switches between:

| Moment | Duration | What he does |
|---|---|---|
| **Idle** | 14 to 30 s | Sitting, he breathes, looks around, blinks, twitches his ears; the tip of his tail sweeps the ground. |
| **Nap** | 25 to 50 s (4 times out of 10) | Curled up, head on the ground, eyes closed. |
| **Flight** | one or two laps | He crouches and leaps, then traces a **big figure eight** 24 m up, at 15 m/s, banking in the turns, and lands exactly where he was. |

His flight always passes **over your villagers' starting point**. He follows the game speed: he stops when paused and races at ×20.

## His size
- Sitting, the top of his head is **11.7 m** high, two and a half times a house's height; 13.1 m with the horns and the top of the wings.
- In flight, his wings span about **25 m**.

:::note[In development]
For now, Zebor is scenery: he blocks neither buildings nor villagers, he cannot be selected, and he does not claim his tribute yet.
:::
