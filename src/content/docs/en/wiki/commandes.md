## Camera
| Action | Control |
|---|---|
| Move the camera | **W A S D** (or Z Q S D depending on keyboard), **arrow keys** |
| Drag the view | hold **middle click** |
| Rotate the camera | hold **right click** |
| Zoom | **mouse wheel** |

## Villagers
| Action | Control |
|---|---|
| Select several villagers | hold **left click** and drag |
| Add to the selection | **Shift** + drag, or **Shift** + click |
| Open a villager, building or resource card | **left click** |
| Give an order | short **right click** on a resource, a building site, a hut or a spot |

### What a right click does
- **Tree, rock, bush**: villagers take the matching job (forester, miner, gatherer) and harvest, then carry on by themselves.
- **Building site**: they become carpenters and work on it until it is finished.
- **Ranger's Cabin, Berry Hut, Woodcutting Workshop**: they are assigned to it, up to the number of places.
- **Anywhere else**: they lose their job and walk to the clicked spot.

## Game
| Action | Control |
|---|---|
| Menu (and pause) | **Esc** or the gear at the top left |
| Quick save | **F5** |
| Quick load | **F9** |
| Game speed | time panel, right of the season strip (×0.5 to ×20) |
