## The start
A new game begins with **4 villagers**, placed near the map's starting flag. **No buildings, no resources.** Every building is available right away in the build bar on the left of the screen.

On the **New Game** screen, choose the map (Picness Valley) then the difficulty:

| Difficulty | Cold sensitivity | Hunger | Fatigue |
|---|---|---|---|
| Easy | 20 % | 20 % | 20 % |
| Normal | 50 % | 50 % | 50 % |
| Hard | 100 % | 100 % | 100 % |
| Custom | your choice | your choice | your choice |

## The first minutes
1. **Plan a storage.** Nothing is harvested while no storage or building site has room for it. Place a [Small Storage Area](/en/wiki/batiments/): it needs no materials, only clearing.
2. **Put your villagers to work.** Select them (hold left click and drag), then right-click a tree, a berry bush or a rock: they take the matching job and carry on by themselves.
3. **Build a house.** Without a house or a cellar, your villagers can neither eat nor warm up. A [Simple House](/en/wiki/batiments/) keeps a little food and firewood.
4. **Get ready for winter.** Firewood comes from the [Woodcutting Workshop](/en/wiki/batiments/), which splits logs. A year lasts only 4 minutes at normal speed.

:::tip
Right-clicking a building site makes the selected villagers carpenters: they work on it until it is finished.
:::
