Welcome to the **Picness Wiki**, the guide to Picness Valley.

Picness Valley is a 3D village game. Four villagers arrive in a valley with nothing: it is up to you to guide them to gather, build and get through the winter warm.

:::note[Game in development]
Picness Valley changes almost every day. This wiki follows **version 0.131**. What's new is listed in the [patch notes](/en/notes/).
:::
